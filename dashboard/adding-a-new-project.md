# Adding a new project

VerifyWise allows users to monitor multiple projects under the same organization.

To create a new project in your organization:

1. Navigate to the Dashboard from the sidebar and click `New project` on the upper right corner of the screen.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (9).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>"Create new project" window</p></figcaption></figure>

2. **Project title:** Give a title to your new project.
3. **Users:** Select the users that will be part of the new project.
4. **Owner:** Choose the person responsible for the project.
5. **Start date:** Enter the commencement date of the project.
6. **At risk classification (High risk/ Limited risk/ Minimal risk):** Enter the correct risk classification for this project, as per the _EU AI Act_.
7. **Goal:** Describe the goal of the project.
8. **Type of high risk role:** Enter the correct role classification for a high-risk AI application.
9. Click `Create project` to complete creating the project.

**NOTE:** The AI risk classification used in the _EU AI Act_ can be found [here](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/high-level-summary/). There are significant and subtle differences between different risk classifications under the _Act_, especially between high-risk and limited-risk AI systems. A thorough understanding of how to classify the risk level of an AI system is paramount to proper AI governance.

**NOTE:** For high-risk AI systems, application deployers and developers are both under additional regulations. However, developers of high-risk AI systems being used in the European Union are under greater scrutiny compared to users (deployers) of high-risk AI systems.
