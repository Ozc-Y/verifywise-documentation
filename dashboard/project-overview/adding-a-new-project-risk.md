# Adding a new project risk

**NOTE:** To add a new vendor risk to a project, please see [Adding a new vendor risk](adding-a-new-vendor-risk.md) instead. This page has instructions for adding a new project risk.

When you decide to add a new risk to your "Project risks" tab, navigate to Dashboard > View project > Project risks > `Add new risk`. The "Add a new risk" window will open.

There are two tabs to fill out: "Risks" and "Mitigation".

\[RISKS IMAGE]

Follow these steps on the "Risks" tab:

1. **Risk name:** Give a name to the new risk.
2. **Risk description:** Provide a brief description of the risk.
3. **AI lifecycle phase:** Choose the correct AI lifecycle phase for the risk.
4. **Action owner:** Designate the person responsible for monitoring and mitigating this risk.
5. **Potential impact:** Describe the potential impact of this risk.
6. **Risk category:** Choose a category for this risk from the drop-down menu.
7. Assign the risk a likelihood level and a severity level. The system will automatically calculate the risk level.
8. Leave review notes. (Optional)
9. Navigate to the "Mitigation" tab.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>"Mitigation" tab</p></figcaption></figure>

Describing the risk itself is not sufficient for compliance with the _EU AI Act_. The measures you are taking to mitigate any risks that your project might pose also need to be thoroughly documented. This is the purpose of the "Mitigation" tab.

On the "Mitigation" tab, follow these steps:

1. **Mitigation status:** Select the present mitigation status of the risk.
2. **Current risk level:** Select the current level of risk.&#x20;
3. **Start date:** Enter the date when the risk became a consideration.
4. **Mitigation plan:** Describe how you plan on mitigating this risk.
5. **Implementation strategy:** Explain how you will enact your mitigation plan, in detail.
6. **Residual risk level:** Enter the risk level again, this time taking your mitigation strategy into consideration.
7. **Risk approval:** Choose an approver (person who approves the chosen mitigation measures), choose an approval status, and enter the approval start date.
8. Leave recommendations. (Optional)
9. Finally, click `Save` .
